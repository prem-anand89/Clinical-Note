Facial Palsy Patient Profile module — not inline in the real note
Pattern & etiology informs interpretation of the grading below
—
Read-only
Peripheral (Bell's palsy pattern) involves the forehead — the LMN lesion affects the whole nerve, so upper and lower face are equally impaired. Central (stroke-related) palsy typically spares the forehead, due to bilateral cortical innervation to frontalis.
Select a grade to see its description.
Eye care
The most urgent safety concern in facial palsy — incomplete eye closure risks exposure keratopathy and corneal injury, and drives referral urgency independent of the overall grade.
Moderate/severe lagophthalmos with no protection in use — flag for urgent ophthalmology referral, not just rehab follow-up.
Sunnybrook Facial Grading
Implemented directly from the official Sunnybrook worksheet — resting symmetry choices, the 5 standard expressions, the ×5/×4 multipliers, and the composite formula all match the published scoring sheet. Worth a clinical spot-check before shipping, same as the other scored instruments, but this is the real formula, not an estimate.

Symmetry of voluntary movement degree of muscle excursion compared to normal side — 1 (unable to initiate) to 5 (movement complete)
Synkinesis degree of involuntary muscle contraction with each expression — 0 (none) to 3 (severe/disfiguring)

Dynamic Gait Index (DGI)
8 items, 0 (severe impairment) – 3 (normal), total /24 — chosen over FGA for this pass; performance-based, complements the bedside findings above.
Dizziness Handicap Inventory (DHI)
Representative 12-item subset (paraphrased), not the full 25-item official instrument — same scoping choice as FAAM's ADL subscale. Raw score only; the official 0–100 severity bands (mild/moderate/severe) assume all 25 items, so aren't applied to this reduced set.
